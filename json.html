About the Ballon d'Or

The Ballon d'Or is an annual football award presented by French news magazine France Football that is widely regarded as the most prestigious individual honor in world football. Established in 1956, it honors the standout footballer who performed best over the previous season.

The purpose of this ranking table is to showcase iconic multiple Ballon d'Or winners and legendary figures across football history. You can click on any table column header to sort the players. Data is sourced from a JSON file.
